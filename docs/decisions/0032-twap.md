# 0032 — TWAP execution (EXE-5)
- **Decision:** a strategy asks for TWAP by setting `OrderIntent.exec_algo = TwapSpec(slices, duration_s)` on a market or limit intent. The engine journals the parent intent but never risk-checks or sends it as one order. Each slice is an ordinary child intent (`tag = twap:<algo_id>:<k>`) that goes through quantization, the RiskManager and the journal on its own ("slices obey risk per slice").
- **Scheduling:**
  - Slices are floored to the lot size, and the remainder goes in the last slice.
  - At most one slice is sent per algo per engine tick (every bar in backtests, the supervisor timer in paper/live).
  - While the engine or the strategy is paused, nothing is sent and the schedule slides back, so a resume never fires a burst.
  - STOP for the strategy, flatten, HALT and kill drop the remaining slices.
  - Flatten orders never use TWAP.
- **Restart:** algos live in memory and are not resumed after a restart. A stale parent is never re-executed, the same policy as stale pending orders (I4). Slices already sent are recovered like any other order.
- **Phase 3 (VWAP, Iceberg):** `exec_algo` is a discriminated union (`kind`) of `TwapSpec | VwapSpec | IcebergSpec`. All three share the engine rules above, via `helm.oms.algos.AlgoBase.next_qty` / `record`.
  - **VWAP** is participation-of-volume: after each new closed bar it sends `participation` (≤ 50%) of that bar's volume, lot-floored. At `duration_s` it sends the remainder, or drops it when `finish = false`.
  - **Iceberg** is limit-only. It posts `display_qty` and posts the next slice only after the visible one is FILLED. A canceled, expired or rejected slice stops it, with a WARN alert, so it never silently re-posts.
